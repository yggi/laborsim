PROFILING BENCH

Mobile-first is a pillar and nothing about it has ever been measured (BOARD.md L-034). This drives the real machine through the real renderer, on this device, and times it — six passes over the same six seconds of the same run, each changing one thing: the pixel count, the prop count, the motion, the view, and finally nothing at all, so that drift over a minute of load shows up as itself.

It takes about a minute and a half and the machine will be driving behind this panel the whole time. Leave the screen on and the page in front — a backgrounded tab stops getting frames and the run stalls. When it finishes, press COPY and send the block back.

What it does not measure is the cab: the cage, the dash, the pods and the levers are DOM over the glass and none of it is on this page.
