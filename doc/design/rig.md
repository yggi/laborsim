# The rig — the training system, and what it is allowed to ask of you

Start here for the fiction and everything downstream of it. `doc/MEMORY.md` § 1.1:
**the whole thing is, in-universe, a Labor design, operation and safety training
system.** The player is not piloting a Labor; they are using the rig that
teaches people to.

That is not flavour, and checking it comes *first*. It already licenses
inspectability, replay, affordable failure, sandbox-as-native, procedural sites
and the entire UI register — so before inventing machinery to justify something,
look here for the licence you may already have.

## Where to go instead

- The rig **may read the machine; the machine knows nothing of the rig.** Kit
  bolted into the cab by a manufacturer is [the cab](cab.md).
- What the machine can physically do is [the machine](machine.md). The rig sets
  the task; it does not move the limit.
- The order the work is being done in, and the argument for it, is
  [`code/roadmap.md`](code/roadmap.md).

## The shape of it

The rig is **the reason failure is affordable**. Every page here exists because
the frame makes something cheap that would otherwise be expensive to justify: a
replay, a reset, a priced mistake, an exercise you can fail without anybody
being hurt. When a design question feels like it needs new machinery, the
answer is often that the frame already permits it.
